Privacy Policy

Updated: October 10, 2026

This policy describes DND Master and its related app pages. Sections marked 3.1.0 describe the upcoming release. The developer is Vladimir Petrov. For data and privacy questions, contact mister.vladika@gmail.com.

Campaigns and other game records are stored on your device and may sync through your personal iCloud. AI generation runs locally. Downloads, external websites, support requests and the companion you enable create separate connections described below.

1. Game records and settings

The app stores your campaigns, characters, notes, encounters, custom NPCs and other game records on your device. Settings and web bookmarks are stored locally. Imported audio and playlists are kept in the app’s file storage.

The developer does not automatically receive the contents of these records. The app contains no advertising networks and does not use this content for advertising tracking.

2. iCloud and backups

When iCloud is available and enabled for the app, game records may sync through CloudKit in your personal iCloud. They are transmitted to Apple under your account settings and Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not have access to your private iCloud database.

This does not mean that every app file syncs: music, downloaded models and game libraries are managed separately. The app also creates local storage backups for recovery. System device backups depend on your iOS and iCloud settings.

3. Library downloads

Starting with the upcoming 3.1.0 release, game system libraries will be delivered as asset packs through App Store infrastructure. Downloads and updates need internet access and available storage. Installed content is read locally. Apple receives technical information needed to deliver files; your campaign content is not needed for a library download.

4. Local AI and models

AI is optional. The built-in local engine uses models you download separately from Hugging Face and related delivery servers. During downloads, the service receives network information such as your IP address and file requests under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Some models may require accepting service terms and providing an access token.

In the upcoming 3.1.0 release, a Hugging Face token you enter is stored in the device Keychain without iCloud synchronisation and is used for download access. You can remove it in the app’s Hugging Face access settings. Do not share the token in support messages.

When a local model runs, prompts and the game data provided to it are processed on the device rather than sent to a cloud AI provider. If you choose the Ollama integration on Mac, the app contacts a local service on that same computer (127.0.0.1); the Ollama service is installed and managed separately.

5. Local network companion

When the game master starts the companion, the app opens a local server on their device. Connected players receive the campaign, character, current-combat and library information available to them; their actions and rolls are sent to the game master’s device. This session does not use a separate developer-hosted server.

The connection uses local HTTP and WebSocket. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network and share the link or QR code only with your players. The game master can disconnect participants and stop the session. The app cannot revoke copies of previously displayed data, screenshots or files that players save.

6. Websites, import and export

When you open a website in the built-in or system browser, or import an NPC from a link, your device connects to that website. The site may process IP addresses, cookies, sign-in details and other information under its own policy. The built-in browser may retain website data; it is not a private-browsing mode.

When exporting a campaign, character or other file, you choose its destination and how to share it. The file contents become available to the selected app, cloud storage or recipient. The DND Master developer does not control those transfers.

7. Support and this website

If you contact the developer by email or Telegram, the selected service and recipient receive your message and any attachments. Other group members may see Telegram group messages. Use email for personal data questions and avoid sharing more information than needed to resolve the issue.

These pages contain no embedded advertising or analytics scripts, data-collection forms or external fonts. GitHub Pages serves the pages and images and may process technical visitor information under the GitHub privacy statement. Visits to the App Store, Telegram and other sites are governed by those services’ policies.

8. Managing and deleting data

Game records, downloaded libraries, models and backups can be deleted in the corresponding app sections. Synced changes may propagate to your other devices. Apple system settings let you manage iCloud data and app permissions.

Deleting the app removes its local file storage but does not guarantee deletion of iCloud copies, system backups, exported files, previously sent messages or Keychain entries. Remove the Hugging Face token in the app and revoke it in your Hugging Face account. Manage copies held by recipients and third-party services separately.

9. Policy updates

This page and the date above will be updated when features or data handling change. The app’s age restrictions are shown in its App Store listing.
