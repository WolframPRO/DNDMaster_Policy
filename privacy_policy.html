Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

DND Master ("the App") is developed by Vladimir Petrov ("Developer", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: DND Master does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account.

1. Information We Collect

DND Master does not collect any personal information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration.

Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports (beyond what Apple collects through its own systems, if you have opted in)

2. Data Storage

All data you create in the App — campaigns, characters, encounters, notes, custom NPCs, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your data is automatically synced to your private iCloud account via Apple CloudKit. This allows your data to be available across your Apple devices. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's infrastructure, and the Developer has no access to your iCloud data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

The App includes a built-in browser (iPad only) that can load web pages from dnd.su. When you use this feature, your interactions with those websites are governed by their respective privacy policies. The App does not track or record your browsing activity.

4. iCloud

The App uses Apple's iCloud services for data synchronization:

  • CloudKit — to sync your campaigns, characters, encounters, and other app data across devices
  • Key-Value Storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) — to sync your app preferences

Your iCloud data is protected by Apple's security and privacy measures. For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your data with any third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud account, you have full control over it:

  • Delete individual items (campaigns, characters, etc.) directly within the App
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App from your device
  • Remove iCloud data through Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Storage

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
