Everything a Dungeon Master Needs

⚔️

Combat Tracker

Full combat system with initiative rolls, turn order, HP tracking, damage controls, and concentration indicators for every participant.

📖

Offline Bestiary

Nearly 3,000 creatures available offline with filters by size, creature type, alignment, and challenge rating. Detailed stat blocks at your fingertips.

📥

NPC Import

Paste a link from dnd.su and the creature is automatically imported with all stats and an HTML card for offline viewing.

🧭

Campaigns & Notes

Manage campaigns, add player characters with full HP tracking, and keep sortable notes for key events and details.

📦

Encounter Builder

Pre-build encounters with creatures from the bestiary, then launch combat directly from your campaign with one tap.

🧙‍♂️

Character Pregens

Create player character templates and quickly add them to any campaign. Each campaign copy tracks its own HP independently.

🎲

Dice Roller

Quick and flexible dice rolling screen with a clean interface designed for the game master. Roll any combination on the fly.

🌐

Built-in Browser

On iPad, browse dnd.su directly inside the app — races, classes, bestiary, formulas. Save your favorite pages as bookmarks.

☁️

iCloud Sync

All your campaigns, characters, encounters, and preferences sync seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud.
