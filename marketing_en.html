Your edition’s library

Rules within reach.
Even offline.

Version 3.1.0 lets you choose D&D 5e 2014 or 2024 and download its library. Once installed, search and read the available creatures, spells, items and articles offline.

D&D 5e · 2014D&D 5e · 2024

Switching editions separates campaigns and game material. Entries retain their source links and provenance information. Library content is in Russian.

Library contents differ. Adventure, NPC and loot generators are available for the 2014 edition; they are not automatically available for 2024. Downloads and updates require internet access.

Players only need a browser.

Start the local companion and invite players with a QR code. They do not need to install DND Master or create a separate account.

  1. 01

  2. 02

Optional local AI

Help with ideas when you need it.

On compatible devices, download a model to use the AI assistant while preparing. Generation runs locally; you do not need to send your campaign to a cloud AI service.

Models are separate downloads and need additional storage. Available features depend on the device, model and library. Check AI answers before using them; the game master makes the final call.

Questions

Before your first session

Support on Telegram ↗
What works offline?

Campaigns, combat tracking, dice, built-in sounds and imported audio. Library content works after installation; local AI works after a compatible model is downloaded. The companion needs a shared local Wi-Fi network. Downloads, iCloud and external websites require internet access.

Do I need both libraries?

In version 3.1.0, no. Download the edition you play. You can add the other later. The app shows download size and storage information.

Is AI required to use the app?

No. Campaigns, music, dice and combat tracking work independently of AI. Install a model separately if you want the assistant and your device supports it.

How do I move a campaign?

Use campaign and character file import and export. Game records may also sync through your personal iCloud when it is available and enabled for the app. Audio files and downloaded models are stored locally.

Where can I ask a question or report a problem?

Visit the DND Master community on Telegram or email the developer. Include your app version, device model and steps that led to the issue. Avoid sharing personal campaigns or secrets unless necessary.
